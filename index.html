Crown Change by XtraOrdinary Solutions

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Last updated November 5, 2026

Crown Change is a self-tracking tool for perimenopause and menopause. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have. It applies to the Crown Change mobile and web application published by XtraOrdinary Solutions.

The short version. Your health entries belong to you. We do not sell your data. We do not use your symptom history for advertising. You can delete your entries or your account at any time.

1. Information you give us

  • Account information: email address and authentication details needed to sign in.
  • Health and wellness entries: daily check-ins, symptoms, energy, mood, food and nutrition entries, practice completion, and any notes you write.
  • Voice notes: if you use the optional voice feature, the audio you record is converted to text so you can review and edit it. A draft is not saved to your history unless you confirm it.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, and app version, used for compatibility and crash diagnosis.
  • Basic usage events such as screens opened, used to understand which features work.
  • We do not request precise location and do not collect advertising identifiers for ad targeting.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: save your entries, show your patterns, and personalize guidance.
  • To maintain and improve reliability and fix defects.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share health entries with advertisers, data brokers, or social networks.

4. Where your data is stored and who processes it

Crown Change is built and hosted on the Base44 platform, which provides our database, authentication, and file storage. Data is stored in the United States. Platform service providers process data only on our instructions and only to deliver the service.

If you use the optional voice note feature, your recording is sent to a speech-to-text service to produce a text draft. The audio is used for that conversion and is not used to train third-party models.

5. Health information and the law

Crown Change is a consumer wellness and self-tracking product. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and is not intended to be used in medical emergencies. Information you enter is health-related and is treated as sensitive. We apply access controls so that your entries are visible only to your account.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your entries inside the app at any time.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual entries, or request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Export: you may request a copy of your data by email.
  • Voice feature: it is optional and can be ignored entirely without limiting any other feature.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We honor verified requests regardless of location.

7. Children

Crown Change is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on access-controlled infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your device locked.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
